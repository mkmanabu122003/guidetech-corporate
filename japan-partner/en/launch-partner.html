Contract model

A monthly partner engagement. Scope, response windows, communication channels and included deliverables are agreed in writing before kickoff, and the monthly fee is quoted against that scope rather than taken from a rate card. If the volume changes materially, we revisit the scope with you instead of silently absorbing it or letting quality slip. Commercial terms are confirmed at scoping, after the free readiness call.
